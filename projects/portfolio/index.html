Role-Aware Virtual Agents

ACM Transactions on Graphics, presented at SIGGRAPH 2026

The same virtual robot plays hide-and-seek on an outdoor campus path under three roles: playful, hostile, and supportive. For each move, a multimodal language model reads the scene, the player's position, and the role and picks the agent's next destination; a path planner then shapes the route to express the role. In an outdoor HoloLens 2 study with 27 participants, people perceived the agent differently from one role to the next.

Role
Lead author. I designed the system, built the language-model reasoning and the decision filter, implemented path planning and the AR application, and ran the outdoor user study.
Medium
I built it in Unity for outdoor AR on HoloLens 2, with a multimodal language model.
With
Changyang Li, Cuong Nguyen, Lap-Fai Yu

022024

Dragon's Path

ACM SIGGRAPH 2024 Conference Papers

Standing by a campus pond with an AR headset, a viewer watches a dragon swim, drink, and sleep at spots that suit each action. A predictor trained on human ratings decides which spot suits which action, and an optimizer links the chosen spots with one continuous flight path that stays visible and readable from where the viewer stands.

Role
Lead author. I ran the annotation study, built the location compatibility predictor and the path optimization, implemented the AR application, and ran the outdoor user study with 25 participants.
Medium
I built it in Unity for outdoor AR on HoloLens 2, with character animation and optimization.
With
Rawan Alghofaili, Changyang Li, Lap-Fai Yu

Narrative-Driven Agent

Ready, State, Action (working title), planned submission to SIGGRAPH 2027

Each time the player acts, the story moves forward. From that action and the story so far, a narrative-state planner trained on states extracted from films picks the next interaction and state transition; a multimodal language model then writes the character's dialogue and a spatial goal, and the character moves to that goal in the scene. Across the session, the interaction keeps a coherent arc from setup to resolution.

Role
Lead author. I designed the narrative-state model, built the film-to-state data pipeline, and implemented the simulation, VR, and AR versions.
Medium
I built a simulation of the agent in a virtual environment, a VR example, and an AR mode that overlays only the characters on the physical space.
With
Lap-Fai Yu

Haptic VR Painting and Fabrication

Journal of the Korea Computer Graphics Society, 2020

A painter works inside a 3D reconstruction of Van Gogh's The Bedroom, using a force-feedback stylus that renders contact with each surface as the brush touches it. The painted scene was then fabricated as a monochrome PLA print and a full-color print.

Role
Lead author. I implemented the haptic rendering, the collision detection between the brush and surfaces, and the VR painting system. I painted the scene and produced both prints.
Medium
I worked in VR with force-feedback haptics, then moved to 3D printing.
With
Young J. Kim

052021

Region-Controllable Face Synthesis

Computer Graphics International 2021

An artist can reshape one part of a 3D face, such as the nose, mouth, or cheeks, while the rest of the face stays coherent. The generative model behind this divides face geometry into semantic regions, and a graph neural network models how the regions depend on one another.

Role
Lead author. I designed, trained, and evaluated the model.
Medium
I worked with 3D face geometry, using a generative model and a graph neural network.
With
Young J. Kim

Crafting Dynamic Virtual Activities

Given a 3D scene such as a living room or a restaurant, a multimodal language model writes activities for several characters at once, like a game of darts or a dinner service, and an optimizer places each character to fit the furniture and the others.
